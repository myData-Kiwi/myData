A panel that rises from the bottom of a phone screen, over a scrim, for a short task that returns to the screen beneath: editing a record, or picking one value for a field.

- **Phones only** (under 600px). On desktop the same tasks are an Inspector (editing), a popover (DatePicker, TimePicker) or a Select's list.
- **Use it for** the Inspector's form on a phone (the editor sheet), and for the list, calendar or time list of a Select, DatePicker or TimePicker on a phone (a picker sheet).
- **Do not use it** for a question or a confirmation (Dialog, which is centred on a phone too), for saying that something happened (Toast), for a menu of actions (Menu, by long-press), for a long list that needs search (Select's long-list variant pushes a full page), or to go somewhere (push a page).

- **Two kinds, one shape.** Both have `radius-xl` top corners, square bottom corners, `shadow-2`, and a grabber: a pill `icon-tile` wide and `space-1` tall, in `line-strong`, centred `space-2` from the top. The grabber is decoration; a drag works anywhere on the header.
  - **Editor sheet** (the Inspector on a phone): almost the full height. It leaves the status bar and `space-3` of the screen behind it showing at the top, so the user sees where they came from. Its ground is `surface`, the Inspector's panel. Header, in one `control-height` row under the grabber: Cancel (plain) at the start, the title in the `heading` style in the middle, Save (filled) at the end. The body scrolls under the header with `space-4` padding across; a 1px `line` appears under the header while the body is scrolled under it. There is no footer, so the on-screen keyboard never covers Save.
  - **Picker sheet** (Select, DatePicker, TimePicker on a phone): as tall as its content, at most the editor sheet's height, then its rows scroll. Its ground is `surface-raised`, the ground of the list or popover it stands for, so in dark mode it separates from an editor sheet beneath it. Header: Cancel (plain) at the start, the field's name as the title in the middle, and at the end either nothing or Done (plain, `body-strong`), see below. The body has the rows (`row-height`, with the check mark on the chosen one) or the calendar, and ends above the home indicator's safe area.
- **Done, or a tap.** A picker that holds only choices picks on a tap and closes: no Done. A picker that holds typing (TimePicker's field, Select's custom label) has Done, which commits what was typed and closes; tapping a row still picks and closes. Done is plain, not filled, because the main action of a picker is the choice itself, and an editor sheet beneath already has the filled Save.
- **The scrim.** A `scrim` covers the screen behind. Tapping it does what Cancel does: a picker closes with no change; an editor sheet without changes closes; an editor sheet with changes asks first (below).
- **Leaving: drag, back and Cancel.** Dragging the sheet down follows the finger; released past a third of its height, or flicked down, it is Cancel. The system back gesture and the back button are Cancel too.
  - **With pending input** (an editor sheet with changes, or a picker with typed text not yet committed) the sheet never closes and then asks. A drag down resists: the sheet moves at most `space-8`, springs back on release, and the Dialog "Discard changes?" opens over it (Inspector, "Leaving with changes"). Back, Cancel and the scrim open the same Dialog. "Keep editing" returns focus to where it was.
- **Stacking.** A picker sheet may open over an editor sheet (a DatePicker in the event editor). The editor sheet stays where it is, under a second `scrim`, and the picker rises over it. At most two sheets: a picker never opens another sheet. The one editor-like sheet that may stack is myCalendar's "Custom repeat" (Dialog's one exception; phase 6), which then holds its own pickers in place, not as a third sheet. Closing the top sheet returns to the one beneath, with focus on the field that opened it.
- **The on-screen keyboard.** When it opens, the sheet's body shrinks to the space above it and scrolls so the focused field sits above the keyboard; the header never moves and never goes under the status bar. A picker that holds typing puts its field at the top of its body, under the header, so the keyboard never covers it; its rows scroll under the field.
- **States.** The sheet has no hover, press or focus of its own; its controls keep theirs. While its holder works (Inspector, "Saving"), the header's buttons are inert, not disabled, so focus stays.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.**
  - It is modal: on opening, focus moves into it (the first field of an editor sheet, the chosen row or day of a picker), stays inside it, and the screen behind, including an editor sheet beneath a picker, is hidden from screen readers.
  - The title is announced politely when it opens ("Edit contact", "Start date").
  - Cancel is always visible, so a drag is never the only way out. VoiceOver's escape gesture (a two-finger scrub) and TalkBack's back are Cancel, with the same check for changes.
  - With a hardware keyboard, Escape closes anything open inside first, then is Cancel (platforms.md, "Which handler gets a key first"). On closing, focus returns to what opened the sheet.
- **Motion.** It rises with the `spring` motion and the scrim fades in over `slow`. While dragged it follows the finger; released, it settles with `spring` or leaves downward over `base` with the `standard` curve, the scrim fading with it. A resisting drag springs back with `spring`. With reduced motion it fades in and out over `fast` without rising; a drag still moves it under the finger, because the user is moving it, but release fades it instead of sliding.
- **Light and dark.** In light mode both kinds are white on a dimmed screen, and the picker over an editor separates by its `shadow-2` and the second scrim. In dark mode `surface-raised` is lighter than `surface`, so a picker reads as above the editor without its shadow, and the `scrim` is darker.
- **Kiwi and Graphite.** Only Save (filled) takes the theme's colour, and the check mark and Done take `accent`: kiwi green in Kiwi, near black or near white in Graphite, where the check mark itself, not its colour, marks the choice (Select).
- **Sizes.** One per kind. Buttons are `control-height`; picker rows `row-height`; `body` at 16px, as on every phone screen.
- **Copy.** The title names what is edited or picked, in sentence case with no full stop: "Edit contact", "New event", "Start date", "Calendar", "Label for phone 1". Buttons: "Cancel", "Save", "Done".
- **Used by:**
  - myContacts new contact and edit contact on a phone (editor sheet; Inspector, phase 3).
  - myCalendar new event and edit event on a phone (editor sheet; Inspector, phase 6), with "Custom repeat" stacked over it.
  - The label picker on each phone, email and address, Calendar and Repeat (picker sheets; Select), the dates (DatePicker, including the birthday's Month and Year) and the times (TimePicker, with Done), on a phone.
- **Token needed:** none. The grabber's size comes from `icon-tile` and `space-1`.
- **Why a sheet, and why the scrim cancels.** Editing on a phone is a sheet, not a pushed page: Cancel and Save stay in view, the record behind shows where the user came from, and a sheet is plainly modal, where on a pushed page the back gesture would be ambiguous (does going back save or discard?). Tapping the scrim is Cancel, with the check for changes, as phone sheets usually behave; a Dialog's scrim does nothing, because a Dialog asks a question, while a sheet is a place the user went to and can leave.
- **Decided 2026-10-14:** editing on a phone is the editor sheet; tapping a sheet's scrim is Cancel, with the check for changes.

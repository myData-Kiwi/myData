Where to get a file

Contacts on a MacEvery account on the Mac, with notes and photos
  1. In Contacts, choose Contacts > Settings > vCard, and turn on “Export notes in vCards” and “Export photos in vCards”.
  2. Select all contacts (⌘A in the list).
  3. Choose File > Export > Export vCard…, and save the file.
  4. Drop it here.

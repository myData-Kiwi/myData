A GroupedList row for one event on one day: when, what, where and which calendar.

- A GroupedList row with no icon tile: at least `row-height` tall, with `space-4` padding across, `space-2` down and `space-3` between its parts.
- **When** comes first, in a column of fixed width so that titles line up: the start time in `body`, and the end time under it in `caption` and `ink-muted`. An all-day event shows "All day". Times follow the locale's 12-hour or 24-hour clock.
- **Time zones.** Times are shown in this device's time zone, and an event falls on the days it covers in that zone. While the host is localhost-only, the device is the host, so this is also the host's zone; on a phone or away from home later, it is wherever the user is. An event set in a time zone that differs from the device's adds its times in that zone, first in its caption, with the zone's name in the locale's generic form: "10:00 to 11:00 New York time". An all-day event is a date with no time zone: it stays on the same dates wherever the device is and never gains a zone.
- **An event over several days** appears on each of its days. On its first day it shows its start time over "Starts", on its last day its end time over "Ends", and on the days between "All day". An all-day event over several days shows "All day" on each.
- Next comes a DataColour bar, as tall as the text beside it.
- The title is in `body-strong` and wraps to two lines at most. An event with no title shows "No title" in `ink-muted`.
- Under the title, in `caption` and `ink-muted`, joined by " · ": the event's own times when its zone differs, "Day 2 of 3" for an event over several days, the location if there is one, and the calendar's name. The calendar's name is always there: it is what the colour stands for, here and in the MonthGrid above.
- The row is not pressable, because there is no event page yet. Its text can be selected.
- **In a day list:** one GroupedList per day, headed in the `label` style by the full date ("Wednesday 14 October", with the year when it is not this year). Today's heading starts "Today, ". All-day events come first, then by start time. Events in hidden calendars are left out.
- **A day with nothing on it** has one row, "No events", in `ink-muted`, but only when myData reads that day from every source with a shown calendar. A day outside a source's range never says "No events". It says what is not shown instead: "Events from this Mac are not shown for this day." When the day has events from another source, they are listed and this line follows them. The Notice above the list gives the range.
- You provide the times and the event's time zone (none for an all-day event), the title, the location, the calendar's name and colour, and which day of how many this is.

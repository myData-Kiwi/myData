A small tonal chip that states a status in a word or two.

- A pill, 26px tall, with a status `-soft` fill and text in the matching status colour: `success` on `success-soft`, and so on. Inactive states use `surface-sunken` with `ink-muted`.
- The host's running state is the one exception: a `surface` chip with a `brand` dot. This is the only place the kiwi green appears in the interface.
- The word is always present. Colour is never the only signal.
- One chip per row. Do not stack several statuses side by side.
- You provide the word and the status.

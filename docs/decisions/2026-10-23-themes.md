# Themes that change the whole look (2026-10-23)

Decision record. Where it differs from CLAUDE.md, CLAUDE.md wins. The design is `design/themes/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the format `design/themes/theme.schema.json`, the rules for every theme `design/theming.md`.

## The owner's request

A design followed (20 prototype themes with HTML previews, a file format, a validator prototype, a contrast critique) and was approved in principle.

## Decisions (the owner, 2026-10-23)

- **Each app has its own theme, plus "Use everywhere".** An app uses its own choice, else the "everywhere" theme, else System. Use everywhere sets the "everywhere" theme and clears every per-app choice, with an Undo toast.
- **Theme choices are per device and not synced.** They live in `<home>/device/settings.json` (`themes.apps`, `themes.everywhere`). The host's `/appearance` is retired in phase 2 (GET kept one release, PUT 410). Light, dark or match system stays one setting per device for all apps.
- **System is the default theme:** the platform's own look. On macOS it is the Mac shell, unchanged. Elsewhere it is Kiwi in the platform's font until that platform's own pass, and the picker says so.
- **Custom themes** can be created (duplicate and edit), imported and shared as `.mydatatheme` files, as in Warp. That is phase 5, not now.
- **All 11 choices in the design's summary are accepted as recommended:**
  1. Built-in theme files are CC0-1.0, so a shared duplicate carries no AGPL obligation.
  2. Fonts are bundled as Latin subsets in every app, capped (a test fails above 6 MB per app), not served by the host.
  3. No background images in theme files until phase 6; decide then.
  4. New built-ins are built in batches, and any that does not earn its place in the real app is cut.
  5. A new disabled style for filled buttons in every theme, Kiwi included: `surface-sunken` with an `ink-muted` label (about 4.5:1), since 45% opacity leaves the label at 1.4–1.6:1.
  6. Kiwi's visible changes from the contrast audit (link colour, glass at 80–84% instead of 72%) land in phase 3.
  7. When the accent is close to red, Approve and filled primary buttons are drawn in `ink`, so Approve never looks like Delete.
  8. Upper case only on label and caption roles that never hold a name.
  9. Layout stays out of themes. Folding details away or opening the calendar on the day list is a later device accessibility setting.
  10. Mode stays per device, not per app; phones get one theme per app, with no Use everywhere, until native phone apps exist.
  11. View > Theme… on ⌥⌘T, unless Apple's own apps use it. **Checked 2026-10-23 (phase 2): they do** (Show or Hide Toolbar in Finder, Mail and the other apps with a toolbar), so Theme… shipped with no key; the owner then picked ⌃⌘T ("Open for the owner", decided).
- **Order:** the themes foundation (phases 0 to 3) comes before the phones (M1b).
- **Which new themes to build** was the owner's pick from the gallery: all twenty ("Open for the owner", decided).

## What a theme can change

| Group | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Colours (per palette) | 43 roles in light and dark. Six seeds are required (`bg`, `surface`, `ink`, `ink-muted`, `accent`, `on-accent`); the rest are derived in OKLCH, moving lightness only until each contrast pair passes |
| Increase contrast | Optional overrides; otherwise derived at 7:1 |
| Type | Display, body and mono stacks from a catalogue of OFL fonts; base size 12–19 px; scale; per-role size, weight, line height, tracking, stack and case (upper on label and caption only); tabular figures; italic secondary text |
| Shape | Round, continuous, cut or square corners; five radii; pill or matching controls |
| Density | Compact, regular or roomy, or exact control, row, icon and spacing values per desktop and phone; platform floors applied at run time |
| Borders | Width, style, colour and where they appear (cards, controls, separators, a rule under the title) |
| Elevation | None, soft, hard, offset, bevel or glow; strength; collapse on press |
| Materials | Window ground (solid, gradient, pattern; images from phase 6), sidebar and bars (solid, translucent, blur) |
| Icons | Set (Lucide, Phosphor, Tabler, Material Symbols, Pixelarticons, Iconoir), weight, filled when selected, tile style |
| Motion | Personality (none, calm, standard, crisp, lively), speed, press feedback, entrance |
| Components | How each role is drawn: primary and secondary buttons (a primary is never text only), list, sidebar, toolbar, inputs, selection, avatar, tabs, chat, month, today marker, calendar colours or patterns |

## What stays out of themes

- Wording and sentence case; the sections and their order; the order of actions; keys and the macOS menu bar.
- Layout (decision 9 above).
- The window's controls and the system's file, share and permission dialogs. A drawn theme on a Mac keeps the native window, traffic lights, menu bar and the skeleton (sidebar, list, record, toolbar band); everything inside comes from the theme.
- `brand`, the logo and the kiwi artwork colours; what a status colour means (`danger` stays within 25° of today's red).
- The approval card's structure: it takes the theme's colours and fonts only, on a solid surface with a 1 px `line-strong` edge.
- Mode (per device). Theme files hold no URLs, code or CSS, and nothing is ever fetched.

## Where the design is

- **In the repo** (`design/themes/`): `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `theme.schema.json`, and `<slug>/theme.json` and `RULES.md` for System and the six (`system`, `kiwi`, `hairline`, `frosted`, `journal`, `sticker`, `paperback`), each drawn one with three palettes. The six are the phase 3 targets; phase 1 still builds from `tokens.json` and `themes.json`, because the contrast audit moved some of their values (Frosted's, Sticker's and Paperback's `accent-soft`, Kiwi's link and glass).
- **Not in the repo** until each is built: the 13 other prototype themes (High Contrast, E-ink, Large, Terminal, Notebook, Brutalist, Retro Desktop, Swiss, Blueprint, Arcade, Geometric, Zen, Ledger; their folders keep the old names, mapped above) with their `theme.json` and `RULES.md` in the format before palettes, every theme's HTML previews, the gallery sheets and the palette sheets of the seven built-ins, the example and must-fail files, the prototype validators (the palette validator among them) and the research notes. They are kept with the design session; each moves into `design/themes/<slug>/` with its rules as it is built.

## What this ends

- The theme stored on the host for every app and device (`PUT /appearance`, `/status`'s `theme`), from phase 2.
- "A theme replaces three tokens and nothing else" (`theming.md`), from phase 3; `themes.json` goes then.
- "Everything is rounded, almost no borders" (`README.md`): now Kiwi's rule only.
- "No uppercase labels" as a rule for every theme: now Kiwi's and most themes', with decision 8 for the rest.
- Settings' "Accent" row in the plan (`docs/2026-10-23-plan.md`, section 5): replaced by the theme per app.

## Open for the owner: decided (the owner, 2026-10-23)

- **All twenty themes ship as built-ins,** each built properly with 2 to 4 palettes: System, the six, and the 13 prototypes (High Contrast, E-ink, Large, Terminal, Notebook, Brutalist, Retro Desktop, Swiss, Blueprint, Arcade, Geometric, Zen, Ledger). The prototypes predate palettes and the Dart validator, so each is ported, not copied: it is split into a style and palettes, passes the validator in light and dark and at increased contrast, and keeps layout out (Large keeps no reflow; Ledger keeps only a list or table component variant). Decision 4 (batches, cut what does not earn its place) still applies.
- **Kiwi in an old colour moves.** In phase 3 a choice of Kiwi in Graphite, Dusk, Plum, Rose or Amber moves to that colour's theme in that palette (Kiwi and Plum becomes Journal and Plum; Graphite goes to Hairline, Dusk to Frosted, Rose to Sticker, Amber to Paperback), and myData's Appearance says so once (built 2026-10-24; after the review each moved app also says it once in its own window). The five ids stay readable as hidden aliases, so no stored choice falls back silently (`design/themes/ARCHITECTURE.md` 1.6, 4.2).
- **No "System, tinted" on macOS:** System always follows the Mac accent. Other platforms may allow a tint later (phase 5 or that platform's pass).
- **View > Theme… is ⌃⌘T,** since ⌥⌘T is Show or Hide Toolbar in Apple's apps. On macOS only: on Windows and Linux Control stands for Command, and Ctrl+T is myCalendar's Go to Today, so they reach the gallery from myData's Appearance until they get a key of their own.

## Not verified

The system font names on Windows and Linux; the font bundle size; whether `FontLoader` can reload a family; whether `FontWeight` drives variable-font weights on every engine; how `RoundedSuperellipseBorder` looks on the web; App Groups on a free Apple account. Nothing on screen yet proves a theme works in Flutter: the previews were HTML.

## Phase 1, as built (2026-10-23)

- Nothing changed on screen: every golden and app screenshot is byte-identical, also when regenerated.
- **Increased contrast is verified** for all 18 built-in palettes: the 12 without their own set pass with the derived 7:1 values.
- **Today's look fails three checks the contrast audit found,** which the validator reports and phase 3 fixes (decision 6): Kiwi's glass at 72% under `ink-muted`, and the red focus ring on a Rose or Amber selection. The validator allows Kiwi's six palettes (the schema allows built-ins 2 to 4) until phase 3.
- **Chosen while building** (the owner may revisit): a role's style is `context.typeScale.body` (`context.type(role)` keeps the Mac mapping); a widget's look-dependent measures are static methods taking the look (`Card.gap(context.look)`); unknown keys are left out at every depth; a derived focus ring falls back to today's blue where red would be lost on the selection; derived glass is the palette's surface made opaque enough for text; the density class is the platform's. Details: `design/themes/ARCHITECTURE.md` 3.1, "As built".

## Phase 4, batch 1, as built (2026-10-24)

- **High Contrast, E-ink and Large** are built-ins, each ported from its prototype with three palettes (High Contrast: Blue, Yellow, Cyan; E-ink: White, Cream, Grey; Large: Blue, Green, Purple), passing the validator in both modes at both contrast levels, and each keeping its own stricter promise in a test: 7:1 text for High Contrast and E-ink, no colour and no motion for E-ink, bigger type and targets with the same layout for Large. ## Phase 2, as built (2026-10-23)

- **Each app has its own theme and palette on each device** (`ThemeController` in mydata_core, the `themes` key of `device/settings.json`), with Use everywhere and Undo, in a gallery in every app (View > Theme…) and in myData's Settings > Appearance. Details: `design/themes/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Phase 2 as built".
- **The host's theme is retired:** `PUT /appearance` answers 410; `GET /appearance` and `/status`'s `theme` stay one release, deprecated, read once per device by the migration. The installed host must be rebuilt and installed again.
- **First launch has no theme step** any more, on any platform.
- **⌥⌘T is taken** by Show or Hide Toolbar in Apple's apps, so View > Theme… shipped with no key; it is now ⌃⌘T (decided above).
- **Until phase 3 the gallery offers System and Kiwi in six palettes**, so no choice changes a look beyond today's six colours; on a Mac a drawn theme keeps the Mac window, and the Mac widgets keep their type, with its palette drawn inside.
